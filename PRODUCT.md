# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams and prospective clients are equally important. Hiring teams assess Adrian Cristache for a role, and prospective clients assess him for a project. Both review his experience and work to decide whether to contact him.

## Product Purpose

Aristache is Adrian's personal portfolio. It brings together his enterprise IT experience, PowerShell automation and script security work, and independent web and mobile projects. A successful visit gives visitors enough evidence to assess his fit for their role or project and a clear way to get in touch.

## Operating Context

Adrian manages public content and homepage section visibility through an authenticated admin area. Published content changes take effect without a redeploy.

## Brand Commitments

The portfolio uses the name Aristache and identifies its owner as Adrian Cristache, IT Specialist and Web & Mobile Developer. The existing Aristache monogram is kept in the site's brand assets.

## Evidence on Hand

The site presents experience, skills, selected projects, and testimonials when enabled. Featured projects can show screenshots and GitHub repository statistics. GitHub and LinkedIn provide additional paths to verify work and professional history.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant experience and work easy for both audiences to find and assess.
- Ground claims in published experience, projects, and other available evidence.
- Keep the route from reviewing work to contacting Adrian clear.
